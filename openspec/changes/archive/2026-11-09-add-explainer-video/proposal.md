# Proposal

## Why

A short video shows what Event PayNow Bot does faster than the landing page text, to both audiences: organizers deciding whether to use it, and attendees who receive a QR code or a link and want to know what to do. The owner will publish it on YouTube.

## What Changes

- A **captioned, silent explainer video** (~75 s, 1920x1080 H.264 MP4) with two chapters: **Event Organizer** (create an event, share the payment page or issue QR codes, mark payments received, check attendees in, pay back) and **Attendee** (open the link or receive the QR, pay in a banking app, keep the QR for check-in).
- It is **generated by a script in the repo** (`npm run video`): animated HTML scenes rendered frame by frame with the headless browser already used for checks, encoded with ffmpeg. The script can be re-run whenever the bot changes.
- **The video is not stored in Git.** The output folder `out/` is gitignored; the repo keeps only the script and the scenes.
- Besides the MP4 the script writes a poster still, a `captions.srt` (so YouTube can show real captions as well as the burned-in ones) and a `transcript.txt` (for the YouTube description), all from the same caption text.

Out of scope: voice-over or music, translations, uploading to YouTube, and **embedding the video on the landing page** (a later change, once the YouTube link exists).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `explainer-video`: the repeatable build of the explainer video and its companion files.

### Modified Capabilities

(none)

## Impact

- New: `scripts/explainer/` (scenes page, render script), `package.json` script `video`, `.gitignore` entry for `out/`, a test, README section.
- No change to the Worker, the Pages site, the database or any deploy; nothing is committed that grows the repo.
